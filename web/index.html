Equation and limitations

Male: (137 − age) × (0.285 × weight + 12.1 × height²) / (51 × SCr)
Female: (146 − age) × (0.287 × weight + 9.74 × height²) / (60 × SCr)

Weight in kg, height in m, SCr in mg/dL, result in mL/min.

For educational or independent verification use only. Assumes stable serum creatinine; estimation may be inaccurate in critical illness or rapidly changing renal function. Do not use this estimate alone for drug dosing or clinical decisions.

Reference: Salazar DE, Corcoran GB. Am J Med. 1988;84:1053–1060. PMID 3376975. No cookies, analytics, or uploaded data storage.
